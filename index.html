Not Testable (NT)

Use NT when a component cannot be assessed because of an interfering factor. Document the reason. Do not assign a score of 1 or include NT in a numerical total.

Clinical Observations

Reason for Not Testable (NT)

Clinical decision-support aid only. Verify findings clinically. Record individual components. Do not use the total score alone to determine clinical management.
